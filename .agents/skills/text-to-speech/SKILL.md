# Text to Speech
- Compare providers by Brazilian Portuguese quality, latency, streaming, cost, privacy, and licensing.
- Separate response text from speech rendering; support cancellation and interruption.
- Avoid speaking secrets or sensitive details aloud without contextual confirmation.
- Cache only non-sensitive reusable audio with retention controls.
- Test pronunciation, numbers, acronyms, names, and long responses.
