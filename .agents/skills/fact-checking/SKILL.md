# Fact Checking
- Atomize compound statements into checkable claims.
- Search for primary evidence and independent corroboration.
- Check dates, definitions, geographic scope, and whether a source actually supports the claim.
- Label each claim supported, contradicted, mixed, or unverified.
- Quote only what is necessary and preserve context.
- Do not manufacture certainty or treat absence of evidence as proof.
