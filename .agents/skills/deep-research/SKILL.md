# Deep Research
1. Define the research question, scope, freshness needs, and decision criteria.
2. Break it into subquestions and search authoritative primary sources first.
3. Record each source, publication date, retrieval date, relevant claim, and limitations.
4. Compare independent sources; distinguish corroboration from copied reporting.
5. Identify disagreement, missing evidence, and stale claims.
6. Report findings with citations, confidence, and explicit unknowns.
7. Never publish, message, or modify external systems automatically.
