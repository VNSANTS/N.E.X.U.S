# Speech to Text
- Compare local and cloud STT by latency, language quality (including Brazilian Portuguese), cost, privacy, device limits, and streaming support.
- Normalize audio format and handle silence, clipping, noise, and partial transcripts.
- Preserve uncertainty; do not silently convert low-confidence audio into confirmed commands.
- Require confirmation before sensitive actions inferred from speech.
- Test accents, noisy environments, numbers, names, and interruption cases.
