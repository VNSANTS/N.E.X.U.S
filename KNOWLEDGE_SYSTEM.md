# Knowledge System

The knowledge base stores researched, reusable information. It is distinct from personal memory, raw conversations and operational logs.

## Record fields
A knowledge record should support:
- Stable ID, title, summary, topic and tags.
- Individual claims.
- Source references and permitted supporting excerpts.
- Publisher/author and source type.
- Publication/update date when known; retrieval timestamp.
- Confidence with rationale.
- State: proposed, under_review, validated, disputed, stale or rejected.
- Version, superseded-by relationship and review/expiry date.

The concrete schema is selected after the repository audit.

## Layers
1. Source references and permitted extracts.
2. Working notes and extracted claims.
3. Reviewed/validated knowledge.
4. Compact summaries and retrieval indexes.

Do not copy entire copyrighted works. Preserve links, metadata and concise notes consistent with source terms.

## Retrieval
Prefer validated, current and relevant records. Include provenance when useful. Surface uncertainty and conflicts. Never present low-confidence inference as confirmed fact.

## Maintenance
Deduplicate, version, mark superseded claims, revalidate time-sensitive facts, allow correction/rejection and document backup/export/deletion.
