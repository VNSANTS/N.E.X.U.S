# Knowledge Validation

## States
- **Proposed:** collected but unchecked.
- **Under review:** being compared.
- **Validated:** evidence sufficient for intended use.
- **Disputed:** credible sources conflict.
- **Stale:** may have changed.
- **Rejected:** unreliable, irrelevant or contradicted.

## Assess sources
Authority, expertise, primary/secondary status, date, relevance, transparent methods, conflicts of interest, independent corroboration and whether the source cites another source.

## Validate claims individually
Do not assign one blanket score to a long page. Attach source attribution to each claim. Distinguish evidence, interpretation and inference.

## Confidence
Explain confidence using evidence quality, agreement, recency and limitations. A numeric score is never a substitute for review.

## Conflicts
Preserve competing claims and sources; compare dates, definitions and context; prefer primary evidence where appropriate; explain unresolved uncertainty rather than forcing a conclusion.

## Time-sensitive facts
Prices, software versions, laws, schedules and availability need retrieval dates and rechecking before reuse.

## Promotion
Only validated or explicitly user-confirmed information enters trusted knowledge. User preferences may be valid personal context but must not be generalized as objective facts.
